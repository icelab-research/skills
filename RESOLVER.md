# Skill Resolver

| User intent | Skill |
|---|---|
| Cut a podcast/interview into a Reel, TikTok, or YouTube Short; remove dead air or fillers; repair lip-sync; choose the active speaker; clean captions/audio | `dialogue-reels-editing` |
| Create or review Randomly Distributed covers, thumbnails, fonts, captions, lower thirds, motion graphics, or platform layouts | `randomly-distributed-video-brand` |
| Diagnose hum or noise, polish spoken audio, and verify a publishable audio or video render | `studio-audio-verifying` |

Use the brand skill alongside dialogue editing for Randomly Distributed clips. Use the audio skill when dialogue needs repair or final listening verification.

Do not route general music-video rhythm or beat-cutting requests here; use a beat-sync editing skill. Do not route general long-form video assembly here unless the task specifically needs the dialogue constraints above.
