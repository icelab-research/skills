# Skill Resolver

| User intent | Skill |
|---|---|
| Cut a podcast/interview into a Reel, TikTok, or YouTube Short; remove dead air or fillers; repair lip-sync; choose the active speaker; clean captions/audio | `dialogue-reels-editing` |
| Create or review Randomly Distributed covers, thumbnails, fonts, captions, lower thirds, motion graphics, or platform layouts | `randomly-distributed-video-brand` |

Use both skills when editing a Randomly Distributed dialogue clip for publication.

Do not route general music-video rhythm or beat-cutting requests here; use a beat-sync editing skill. Do not route general long-form video assembly here unless the task specifically needs the dialogue or brand constraints above.
